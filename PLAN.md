# ReviewFlow Pro — Review-to-Social Marketing Workflow App

## What We're Building

A premium mobile app that turns 5-star Google reviews into a complete marketing workflow — reply, social posts, and tracking — all in one tap.

---

### **Design & Visual Style**

- **Color palette:** Deep navy blue background, crisp white text/cards, subtle gold accents for highlights and CTAs
- **Feel:** Premium, minimal, luxury — inspired by high-end fintech apps like Coinbase and banking apps
- **Cards:** Rounded corners with subtle shadows on dark backgrounds
- **Typography:** Clean, modern, easy to scan
- **Buttons:** Bold gold accent for primary actions, outlined for secondary
- **Overall:** Uncluttered, mobile-first, designed for busy contractors on the go

---

### **App Icon**

- Dark navy blue background with a subtle gold star/checkmark emblem — communicating reviews + quality

---

### **Screens**

**1. Home Dashboard**

- Greeting with business name from Brand Profile
- Quick-action cards: "New Review Workflow", "Past Reviews", "Brand Profile"
- Mini checklist showing the SOP steps at a glance
- Recent workflow previews

**2. New Review Workflow**

- Large text input for review text (paste or type)
- Customer name field (optional)
- City/Area field (required)
- Service(s) performed — multi-select from preset list (House Washing, Soft Washing, Concrete Cleaning, etc.)
- Tone dropdown (Professional, Warm, Premium, Luxury)
- "Generate All Content" gold button
- Save Draft and Clear options

**3. Generated Content**

- Clean copy-block cards for each output:
  - Google Review Reply
  - Facebook Business Caption
  - First Comment
  - Personal Share Caption
  - Story Caption
  - Image Prompt
- Each block has a one-tap Copy button and Edit option
- Visual posting order guide at top
- "Mark Complete" toggles for each step
- Save to History button

**4. Posting Checklist (SOP)**

- Step-by-step interactive checklist guiding the posting order
- Checkboxes for each action (copy reply → paste in GBP → post on Facebook → etc.)
- Progress bar showing completion
- Feels like a guided assistant

**5. History / Saved Reviews**

- List of past workflows with date, customer, city, service, and status
- Tap to view full details and all generated content
- Search and filter capabilities

**6. Brand Profile / Settings**

- One-time setup for business details: name, owner, website, phone, service area
- Brand voice, tone, style preferences
- Color theme and CTA preferences
- Default keywords and services
- All saved data auto-populates into AI prompts

---

### **Features**

- Paste a 5-star review and generate all marketing content in one tap
- AI generates: Google reply, Facebook business post, first comment, personal share caption, story caption, and image prompt
- One-tap copy buttons for every generated text block
- Step-by-step posting checklist so you never miss a step
- Brand Profile saves your business details so AI uses them automatically
- History stores every completed workflow for reuse
- Tone and service selection customize each generation
- Works offline for viewing saved content
- Mark each step complete to track your progress
